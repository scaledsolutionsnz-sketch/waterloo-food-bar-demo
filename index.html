Our story

Your friendly local food bar

Waterloo Food Bar is a Christchurch takeaway run on a simple idea: good food, cooked fresh, at prices that don't make you wince. No fancy fuss — just the burgers, chips and fried favourites the neighbourhood actually wants.

Jo and the team know most regulars by their order. Phone ahead and it'll be hot and bagged when you walk in, or order through Uber Eats and we'll bring it to you.

  • ✓ Family-run and proudly local to Christchurch
  • ✓ Everything cooked fresh to order — never reheated
  • ✓ Generous portions at honest, everyday prices
