Privacy Policy

How Waterloo Food Bar handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who we are

Waterloo Food Bar is a takeaway based in Christchurch, New Zealand. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website. This is placeholder content for a demonstration website.

2. What we collect

When you contact us or submit feedback we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone)
  • The content of your message, enquiry or feedback
  • Any rating you provide through our review page

3. How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, fulfil your order, and improve our food and service. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. How it's handled

Website form submissions are delivered to us by email through a third-party form service (FormSubmit). Orders placed via delivery platforms are handled under those platforms' own privacy policies.

5. Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and only keep it as long as needed to deal with your enquiry.

6. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request a correction. Get in touch using the details below.

7. Contact
